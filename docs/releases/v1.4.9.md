# LocalFlow 1.4.9

## New: Send to WhatsApp (macOS, opt-in)

- **Dictate a WhatsApp message about whatever is on your screen.** Turn it on
  in **Settings → Dictation → Send to WhatsApp** and enter a phone number with
  its country code (your own number works for notes to yourself). It is off by
  default.
- When no text box is selected, hold your push-to-talk key as usual.
  LocalFlow takes a screenshot of the display the moment you press, transcribes
  and polishes what you say, then sends **one message — the screenshot with
  your words as its caption** — through the WhatsApp for Mac app.
- WhatsApp stays in the background: it never comes to the front, and if it was
  hidden it is hidden again afterwards. Your clipboard is restored.
- The recording indicator shows a paper-plane icon when a dictation is headed
  to WhatsApp, and confirms "Sent to WhatsApp" afterwards.
- If anything goes wrong (WhatsApp missing, number not on WhatsApp, a
  permission not granted), nothing partial is sent: your text is copied to the
  clipboard and kept in history.
- **Send test message** in Settings checks the setup straight away.
- Requires WhatsApp for Mac, signed in, plus LocalFlow's existing Accessibility
  and Screen Recording permissions. WhatsApp must be in English for now; in
  other languages it fails safely as above.

Windows and Linux are unchanged in this release; Send to WhatsApp is macOS-only
for now (see the [feature parity contract](../FEATURE_PARITY.md)).

## Updating

Use **Check for Updates** on macOS or Windows, or the in-app updater for the
recommended Linux AppImage. Debian/Ubuntu DEB users download and install the
new package manually.

## Validation scope

Unit tests cover phone-number validation, text-field detection, the staged
image count and caption limits. The full send was tested on macOS with WhatsApp
26.46 to a self chat with another app in front, plus a failure case that sent
nothing and copied the transcript. Hands-on Windows/Linux testing remains
deferred until those machines are available.
